ClipperAgent

An AI-assisted video workflow application for creators who manage content on TikTok.

What the service does

ClipperAgent helps organize video projects, prepare descriptions and publishing materials, and manage creator-authorized posting workflows. TikTok integration is subject to applicable platform permissions and the user's authorization.

How it works

The application's automation and processing components operate on the operator's Ubuntu system. This website provides public information about the service and its policies; it is not the application's user interface.

Availability

ClipperAgent is currently under development. Features that require TikTok approval are not represented as generally available.

Contact

Service operator contact will be published before production launch.
